**Canon if Episode 1's vote = B — RUN FOR THE SHUTTER.**
Runtime about 88 s · 9:16 · 24 fps · continues from the exact frame where Episode 1 ended.

**What this branch costs and gives:**

| Kai loses | Kai gains |
|---|---|
| Wren's trust, for good unless he earns it back | Street level, Ruth at his side, a fire axe and a master keycard |
| His innocence: he is now "the one who left" | **Voice contact with Lucy** and a promise (phone battery becomes a clock) |
| The belief that the surface is safe | The truth: the city sealed them in *on purpose* |

Neither branch is the "safe" one. Branch B proves the surface is worse than the tunnels.

## Consequences carried forward (canon flags)
- **WREN_TRUST = BROKEN.** She tells the group "He left me." Marcus becomes her protector, and he uses that.
- **TOMAS = LOOSE.** Marcus cut the chain and was **scratched on his LEFT hand** doing it (hidden under a handkerchief; it pays off in Episode 4).
- **RUTH = CLOSE.** She is beside Kai, so the Episode 5 handwriting reveal is a deeper wound.
- **LUCY = VOICE.** A promise made. Battery % ticks down each episode (23 → 17 → 9 …).
- **KAI_ARC = REDEMPTION.** He has to earn back a child's trust.
- **RESOURCES = FIRE AXE + RUTH'S MASTER KEYCARD.**

## Into Episode 3, "Collection"
- **B-A (go back down):** Kai and Ruth use the keycard on the staff stairs to get back below the shutter. They reach the platform at 3:00 as 0417's doors open. Marcus is holding Wren's hand, and Wren refuses to come to Kai.
- **B-B (cross the city):** Ruth stays behind as Kai's voice on the radio. Kai runs the rain-soaked streets to the river and finds an army checkpoint firing on the crowd. He is driven down into **Riverside station**, where 0417 waits, and Marcus and Wren are boarding.
