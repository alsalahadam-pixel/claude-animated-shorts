# THE LAST TRAIN — Episode 1: "Nobody Got Off" (Stage 2: Screenplay)

**Runtime:** 88.0 s · **Format:** 1080×1920, 24 fps · **Shots:** 30 (average 2.9 s) · **Spoken words:** 131 in total, so the pictures carry the story.

Timecodes are `m:ss.s`. The bracketed `[S#]` labels match the shot numbers in `05_ep01_storyboard.md`.
VO = Kai's voice-over (recorded close-mic, intimate, present-tense memory). O.S. = off-screen. (R) = heard over radio or phone.

**Structure:**

| Window | Purpose | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Hook | Doors open on a carriage of motionless people. "Nobody got off." |
| 0:05–0:15 | Who and where | Kai, night cleaner, polite and tired. The Commuter's ear is bleeding. |
| 0:15–0:36 | Danger and objective | PA: services suspended. The radio blast turns every head. The doors trap them, but not for long. Ruth: shutters drop at 2:21. |
| 0:36–0:48 | First discovery | The escalator run. Lucy's voicemail ("someone's knocking"). The goal becomes personal. |
| 0:48–1:01 | Complication | Knocking. A chained door: "DO NOT OPEN. NO MATTER WHAT SHE SAYS." Wren: "A lady locked us in." |
| 1:01–1:09 | Suspense and action | Bolters flood up the escalator. Jangling keys. The shutter is half down. Lucy is calling. |
| 1:09–1:28 | Revelation, decision and cliffhanger | Someone tall is in the room with Wren. Kai asks *you*: open the door or run? |

## COLD OPEN

**INT. HALLORAN CROSS — PLATFORM 2 (LEVEL -3) — 2:17 AM**

**[S1] 0:00.0–0:02.6**

*SFX: brake-hiss tail, the two-tone DOOR CHIME (E5 → C5, the series motif), fluorescent buzz.*

Train doors slide apart, filling the frame. Inside the carriage, under flickering cyan light, about twenty passengers stand with **their backs to us**, packed shoulder to shoulder, faces to the far windows. Swaying. Perfectly silent, except for a faint, many-voiced **hum**. The station clock, reflected in the door glass, reads **02:17**.

> **KAI (VO)** *(low, close, a little hoarse, like remembering something he doesn't want to)*
> The last train came in at two-seventeen.

**[S2] 0:02.6–0:05.0**

From inside the carriage, between two dark out-of-focus shoulders: **KAI MORENO (23)**, small in the doorway on the empty platform. Orange hi-vis vest, round taped glasses, a mop in his hand, one earbud in.

> **KAI (VO)**
> Nobody got off.

**[S3] 0:05.0–0:07.6**

Kai pulls out his earbud (tinny music cuts off) and puts on the polite customer-service smile he's done a thousand times.

> **KAI** *(routine and not loud. He has said it a thousand times.)*
> Last stop, folks.

Nothing. Not one head moves. His smile slips.

**[S4] 0:07.6–0:10.6**

Kai steps toward the doors, mop held like a walking stick. We catch his details: the STAFF print, the "KAI" badge, the key reel at his hip.

> **KAI (VO)** *(dry, almost a joke)*
> Night shift. Usually the worst thing down here is a drunk.

**[S5] 0:10.6–0:13.4**

Close behind **THE COMMUTER**, a woman in a camel coat nearest the door. An earbud in her ear leaks tinny music. In her slack hand her phone vibrates: **"MUM ❤️ calling…"**. She doesn't react. Focus slides up from the phone to her ear and a thin **dark trail of blood** running from it down her neck.

*SFX: phone buzz, tinny earbud music, the hum, slightly louder.*

**[S6] 0:13.4–0:15.4**

Close on Kai, cyan light across his glasses.

> **KAI** *(soft, uncertain)*
> Ma'am…? You okay?

In the soft foreground the Commuter's head **twitches**, ten degrees, toward his voice. Then stops.

**[S7] 0:15.4–0:19.4**

*SFX: the PA CHIME (a different, three-note tone).*

High above: the departure board. Row by row, every line flips with a mechanical clatter to **SUSPENDED**, all except the bottom row, which stays lit: `0417 · NOT IN SERVICE`. The camera cranes down to Kai, tiny beside the long train.

> **PA (V.O.)** *(calm, automated, female, polite)*
> Emergency announcement. All services are suspended. Remain where you are. Help is on the way.

**[S8] 0:19.4–0:21.6**

Inside the carriage, low among the passengers. As the PA speaks, **every head tilts up toward the ceiling speaker, all at once**, with a soft crackle of neck tendons. Their faces stay hidden in backlight.

*(PA line finishes: "…Help is on the way.")*

**[S9] 0:21.6–0:23.2**

Extreme close-up of the radio on Kai's chest. It **BLASTS** at full volume, distorted:

> **RUTH (R)** *(urgent)*
> KAI! Kai, pick up!

Kai's hand slaps it silent. **All sound cuts out.**

**[S10] 0:23.2–0:26.6**

*Total silence. Then a sub-bass swell rises from nothing.*

Over Kai's shoulder: inside the carriage, every passenger **turns**. Not smoothly but in stuttering steps, as if missing frames, until twenty faces stare at him. Grey-green skin. Dark veins. Milky eyes with pinprick pupils. **Blood trails from every ear.** Jaws working silently.

**[S11] 0:26.6–0:28.2**

Close on Kai. He doesn't breathe. Twenty faces reflect in his glasses. He takes **one** step back, and the key reel at his hip **jingles**.

*SFX: the jingle (the second sound), then the DOOR CHIME as the doors begin to close.*

**[S12] 0:28.2–0:30.2**

The Commuter **lunges**. The doors slam shut on her arm. Her clawing fingers stop inches from Kai's face. The whole carriage hits the windows at once: **THOOM**. The glass spiderwebs.

*SFX: door slam, a body-mass impact on glass, crackling glass. Music: a low string hit.*

**[S13] 0:30.2–0:36.2**

Kai backs away along the platform, radio pressed to his ear, whispering. Behind him, out of focus, the windows are full of pounding hands and the cracks spread.

> **RUTH (R)** *(low, fast, a voice that has given orders before)*
> Control's sealing the stations. Shutters drop at two twenty-one. Get out.
>
> **KAI** *(whisper, cracking)*
> There's people on the train—
>
> **RUTH (R)**
> They're not people anymore. Run.

*Music: a pulsing low-string ostinato begins (100 BPM).*

**[S14] 0:36.2–0:37.8**

Insert: the departure board's header line flips to **STATION CLOSING 02:21**. The clock beside it ticks over to **02:18**. Beneath, the `0417 · NOT IN SERVICE` row still glows.

## THE RUN

**INT. ESCALATOR — CONTINUOUS**

**[S15] 0:37.8–0:40.8**

Low angle at the top of the stopped escalator. Kai charges up toward the lens, mop in hand. On the steps, things abandoned in a hurry: a single high heel, a dropped bouquet, a **tipped-over stroller (empty) with a plush rabbit**. Far below, the train windows bulge.

*SFX: sneakers slapping metal steps, ragged breathing, glass groaning far below.*

**[S16] 0:40.8–0:44.8**

Running, Kai pulls out his buzzing phone. The screen: **LUCY 💛 · 14 missed calls · Voicemail (0:09)**. He thumbs it to his ear and slows.

> **LUCY (R)** *(9, a tiny whisper)*
> Kai? Mum's still at the hospital… someone's knocking. He won't stop knocking.

Kai **stops dead**.

**[S17] 0:44.8–0:48.0**

Close on Kai. His lockscreen glows on his face: Lucy, gap-toothed, wearing his hi-vis vest as a dress. His jaw sets.

> **KAI (VO)**
> My little sister. Home alone. Four stops away.

*(The objective is now personal.)*

## THE DOOR

**INT. MEZZANINE CORRIDOR (LEVEL -2) — 2:19 AM**

**[S18] 0:48.0–0:51.0**

Behind Kai as he sprints down the long, low corridor (left to right). At the far end, up a short flight of stairs, the steel **shutter is already grinding down** with amber strobes and a pulsing alarm.

*SFX: shutter motor grind, BEEP… BEEP… The ostinato tightens.*

**[S19] 0:51.0–0:52.6**

*SFX: KNOCK. KNOCK. KNOCK. Small knuckles on steel.*

Kai skids to a stop and turns toward the sound, and the camera turns with him: a steel door on the north wall, **SIGNAL EQUIPMENT · STAFF ONLY**, with a **heavy chain and padlock** looped through its handle.

**[S20] 0:52.6–0:55.4**

The camera tilts slowly down the door. Across it, in dripping red marker:

**DO NOT OPEN.**
**NO MATTER WHAT SHE SAYS.**

> **WREN (O.S.)** *(a whisper from behind the steel)*
> Hello…? Is someone there?

*Music: the strings fall away. A single high glass-harmonic tone and a slow heartbeat remain.*

**[S21] 0:55.4–1:00.4**

At the small wire-glass window, a face rises into the red light: **WREN HALE (9)**. Copper bob, a brass bird clip, huge grey-green eyes, a finger pressed to her lips. Kai leans in close.

> **WREN** *(whisper, very calm)*
> Please… a lady locked us in.
>
> **KAI** *(whisper)*
> …Us?
>
> **WREN** *(a beat too long)*
> …Me. Just me.

## THE SQUEEZE

**[S22] 1:00.4–1:03.0**

*SFX: far below, the train's windows SHATTER, the sound echoing up the escalator shaft.*

Low angle down the corridor, behind Kai (screen-left): the infected **spill over the top of the escalator**. First **THE NIGHT RUNNER**, a reflective jacket, moving in stuttering bursts and dropping to all fours. Then the Commuter, lopsided. Then the shambling crowd. **And they stop**, clustered 30 m away, heads twitching and searching. They have lost him.

**[S23] 1:03.0–1:05.2**

Extreme close-up: Kai's shaking hands rip the key reel out. About thirty keys **JANGLE**, a bright, terrible sound in the corridor. He fumbles for a green tag marked **SIGNAL RM**.

> **KAI** *(whisper, babbling)*
> What's your name?
>
> **WREN (O.S.)**
> Wren.

Deep in the background, out of focus, the Night Runner's head **snaps** toward the jangle.

**[S24] 1:05.2–1:06.6**

Insert: the shutter is now **half down**. BEEP. BEEP. Amber strobe.

**[S25] 1:06.6–1:08.8**

Kai's pocket lights up and **RINGS**: Lucy's ringtone, bright and loud. **LUCY 💛 CALLING.** Kai crushes the phone against his chest, smothering it in his hoodie, eyes squeezed shut. Deep in the background, **every head in the pack turns toward him**, and they begin to creep forward in stuttering steps.

*SFX: ringtone muffled to a thud. Heartbeat.*

## THE REVELATION

**[S26] 1:08.8–1:13.6**

The camera pushes slowly past Kai's shoulder, *into* the little window. Wren in the foreground, finger to her lips. Behind her, deep in the dark room, lit only by blinking equipment LEDs: **a very tall figure standing with its face to the wall. Swaying. Long coat. A train driver's cap.** The hum.

> **KAI** *(barely a breath)*
> Wren… who's in there with you?
>
> **WREN** *(not turning around)*
> …Nobody.

The tall figure's head **tilts**, slowly, toward the door.

*Music and SFX: total silence. Only the hum.*

**[S27] 1:13.6–1:16.0**

Straight down from the ceiling: the whole corridor in one image. **Left:** the pack creeping forward, the Night Runner low in front, coiled. **Centre:** Kai at the chained door. **Right:** the shutter at knee height, sinking. Time slows (speed ramp to 50%) and the strobes stretch. *One more sound and they run.*

**[S28] 1:16.0–1:19.0**

Extreme close-up of Kai's eyes behind the taped glasses, flicking: door → shutter → phone.

> **KAI (VO)** *(quiet, wrecked)*
> Lucy's waiting for me. So is she.

---

## THE CHOICE

**[S29] 1:19.0–1:22.6**

Kai turns his head and **looks straight into the lens**, at *us*. Frightened. Pleading. Twenty seconds older than he was.

> **KAI** *(whisper)*
> What do I do?

**[S30] 1:22.6–1:28.0**

The frame freezes and splits:
- **LEFT, cold red:** the key at the padlock, Wren's eyes in the window. **A — OPEN THE DOOR 🚪**
- **RIGHT, amber:** the shutter gap and the glowing "LUCY 💛 CALLING". **B — RUN FOR THE SHUTTER 🏃**

> **KAI (VO)** *(whisper)*
> Would you open the door? A… or B?

On-screen: **YOUR CHOICE BECOMES PART 2.** The series title **THE LAST TRAIN** appears small at the top.

Then, from behind the door, the chain goes **CLANK** as something inside pulls the handle, hard. *(That is the second sound.)*

**CUT TO BLACK.**

*SFX: in the black, the soft two-tone DOOR CHIME (E5 → C5), which loops straight back into frame 1.*

**END OF EPISODE 1.**

## Performance notes

- **Kai** is never heroic in this episode. He apologises, he fumbles, and every noise he makes has a consequence: radio (they turn), keys on the platform (they lunge), keys at the door (the Runner turns), phone (the pack creeps closer). His only brave act is stopping at the door.
- **The rule of two** is taught four times without a word of explanation. By S27 the audience knows that any sound now means death, and that makes both options in the vote dangerous: the chain is noisy, and so are running feet.
- **Wren** never raises her voice. Her calm is the scariest thing in the episode. "…Nobody." is a child's loyal lie, not a villain's line. Play it as protecting her dad.
- **Ruth** is clipped, practical and loving. "They're not people anymore" should sound like she has already seen something today that she will never tell Kai about.
- **Lucy's** voicemail must sound real: breathy, too close to the mic, the phone rustling.
- **The PA** is perfectly calm. Its politeness is the horror.
