**[A14] 0:48.0–0:56.0 · Count to forty** *(a moment that breathes)*

## Consequences carried forward (canon flags)
- **WREN_TRUST = HIGH.** She calls Kai by name and holds his sleeve when it's dark.
- **TOMAS = LOOSE.** The mop barricade gives way off-screen in Episode 3. He follows the sound of Wren's voice for the rest of the season and becomes the Conductor in Episode 7.
- **RUTH = SUSPECT.** "You opened it" sits in the audience's mind until Episode 5 pays it off.
- **LUCY = TEXT ONLY.** Silent contact. Each unanswered text is a small horror.
- **RESOURCE = DRIVER KEY** (it can start Train 0417's cab).

## Into Episode 3, "Collection"
- **A-A (stop):** the siren wakes the tunnel. 0417 grinds to a halt. Wren's key opens the cab door. Inside the cab: no driver. Behind the cab door: **carriages full of Stillers**. They ride a train of sleepers into Riverside station, where Priya and Marcus are hiding.
- **A-B (hide):** the train thunders past. The waking Stillers chase *the train*, not them. Kai and Wren walk the empty tunnel to Riverside, where 0417 has stopped "to collect", and meet Priya and Marcus there.
