# THE LAST TRAIN

*An interactive animated zombie-survival series for Shorts, TikTok and Reels. The audience votes on what happens next.*

> **"The last train came in at 2:17. Nobody got off."**

Kai, a 23-year-old night cleaner, has four minutes before Halloran Cross station seals shut. His little sister is home alone four stops away. Behind a chained door marked *DO NOT OPEN. NO MATTER WHAT SHE SAYS*, a girl whispers that she's alone. She isn't.

**Episode 1 vote:** A — Open the door 🚪 · B — Run for the shutter 🏃
