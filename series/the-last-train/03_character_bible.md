# THE LAST TRAIN — Character Bible (Stage 3)

Every recurring character has a **LOCK** list: features that must never change between shots or episodes. During QC, check every generated shot against the LOCK list. Side-specific details (LEFT/RIGHT) are from the *character's* point of view. Image generators often mirror these, so check them every time.

Global character style: stylized 3D. Heads about 10% larger than realistic, eyes about 15% larger, hands slightly oversized for expressive gestures, simplified but sculpted facial planes, painterly skin texture with subsurface scattering, readable silhouettes. Adults are about 6.5 heads tall and children about 5.5.

## KAI MORENO (protagonist)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 23 |
| **Job** | Night-shift station assistant (cleaner and closing checks) at Halloran Cross |
| **Build** | 175 cm, lean, slightly round-shouldered (he slouches until he has to be brave) |
| **Skin** | Warm brown `#8D5A3B` |
| **Face** | Soft oval face, strong dark eyebrows, **small pale scar through the LEFT eyebrow**, dark-brown eyes, slight overbite, a dimple on the right cheek when he forces a smile |
| **Hair** | Black, tight curls, longer on top, short faded sides; one curl always falls over his forehead |
| **Glasses** | **Round, thin gold-wire frames with white athletic tape wrapped around the LEFT hinge**. His main silhouette marker. |
| **Clothing** | **Hi-vis orange vest** `#FF6A13` with two horizontal silver reflective bands, "HALLORAN CROSS — STAFF" printed across the back, name badge "KAI" on the RIGHT chest, two-way radio clipped on the LEFT chest strap. Charcoal hoodie `#3A3D42` underneath with the hood down. Navy work trousers. Scuffed white sneakers, **the LEFT shoe has one orange lace** (Lucy re-laced it). |
| **Carried** | Retractable **key reel on the RIGHT hip** with about 30 keys and coloured tags (it jangles, which matters to the story). A wired single earbud in the RIGHT ear at the start. A mop (Ep 1 to Ep 2A). Phone in the LEFT hoodie pocket. |
| **Lockscreen** | Lucy grinning with a gap tooth, wearing Kai's hi-vis vest as a dress at her 9th birthday |

**LOCK:** taped LEFT hinge · LEFT eyebrow scar · orange vest with two silver bands · radio LEFT chest · badge RIGHT chest · key reel RIGHT hip · one orange lace on LEFT shoe · curl on forehead.

**Personality:** wry, gentle, a people-pleaser. He apologises to drunks he's waking up. Jokes when he's scared and talks too fast when he panics. Panic makes him clumsy and **noisy**, which is his fatal flaw in a world where sound kills. He isn't brave by default, but he is stubbornly decent. Six months of night shifts have made him an expert on the station's layout and keys and useless at everything else.

**Voice:** soft mid-baritone with a slight rasp from the night shift. Local working-class city accent. Natural and unpolished. Whispers crack. Avoid "movie hero" delivery.

**Motivation:** get home to Lucy. Deeper: their dad walked out when Lucy was a baby. Kai's whole identity is *"I'm the one who stays."*

**Fears:** being the one who leaves, the dark tunnels (he has never walked them), and failing Lucy.

**Relationships:** Ruth is his work-mum. Lucy is his whole world. Wren becomes either the kid he chose (A) or the kid he left (B).

**Arc (Season 1):** from someone who reacts and apologises to someone who decides and owns his decisions. It mirrors the audience's own journey.

**Expression range to model:** forced polite smile · frozen terror (breath held, pupils wide) · panic-fumble · teary jaw-clench · whispered tenderness · exhausted dark humour · the direct-to-lens "What do I do?" (an open, frightened, pleading face).

## WREN HALE (the girl behind the door)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 9 |
| **Build** | Small for her age, about 5.5 heads tall |
| **Skin** | Pale with cool undertone and freckles across the nose `#F1D3C2` |
| **Face** | Large grey-green eyes (the most expressive in the cast), straight dark brows, small serious mouth. **Always calm when she should be crying**, which unsettles everyone. |
| **Hair** | Straight copper-auburn bob, slightly uneven (self-cut), held on the RIGHT side by a **small brass hair clip shaped like a wren** |
| **Clothing** | Her dad's **oversized heather-grey City Metro staff fleece** (logo on LEFT chest, sleeves rolled four times, hem to her knees), black leggings, **mustard-yellow rain boots** `#E3B23C` |
| **Detail** | **White bandage on her LEFT wrist** (hides the old healed scratch, a season-long clue). Brass **driver's key on a red lanyard** under the fleece (revealed in Ep 2). |

**LOCK:** wren clip RIGHT side · oversized grey fleece with sleeves rolled · yellow boots · bandage LEFT wrist · copper bob.

**Personality:** observant, literal, very quiet. She speaks in short, precise sentences, the way a child talks when she's had to grow up overnight. Fiercely loyal to her dad, which makes her a liar when it comes to him. She learned the Hush rules by sitting next to him in the dark for an hour.

**Voice:** small, clear, *whispered* almost all the time. Never a shrill movie-child scream, except once, in Ep 2A ("DAD!"), and it's a disaster.

**Motivation:** keep her dad safe, and believe he's still in there.

**Fears:** being left alone, and losing him.

**Secret:** she doesn't know she's immune.

## RUTH ADEYEMI (station supervisor)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 58 |
| **Build** | 165 cm, sturdy, upright ex-military posture |
| **Skin** | Deep brown `#5A3A2A` |
| **Face** | Broad cheekbones, laugh lines, steady eyes, **reading glasses on a beaded chain** (green and gold beads) |
| **Hair** | Short grey locs tied back |
| **Clothing** | Navy supervisor blazer with a red City Metro lanyard and a master keycard, white shirt, practical black boots. Fire axe from Ep 2B. |
| **Tell** | **Red marker stains on her RIGHT fingertips** (Ep 2B), a planted clue |

**LOCK:** beaded glasses chain · grey locs · navy blazer with red lanyard.

**Personality:** warm, dry humour, calm under pressure. A former army medic who calls everyone "love". She follows orders because orders have kept people alive before.

**Voice:** deep, unhurried alto, Nigerian-British accent softened by 30 years in the city. On the radio she sounds like an anchor.

**Secret and motivation:** Control promised to evacuate her daughter and grandson from the flood zone if she sealed Halloran Cross "by the book". So she chained a door with a child behind it.

**Arc:** from mentor to betrayer to (by vote) redemption or abandonment.

## Model-sheet checklist (make these first, before any shot)

For each main character (Kai, Wren, Tomas human and Stiller, Ruth, the Commuter, the Night Runner):
1. A turnaround: front, three-quarter, side and back, in neutral grey light.
2. An expression sheet with 6 faces.
3. A colour swatch strip with the hex codes above.
4. A silhouette test: the character filled pure black has to stay recognisable.
5. A lighting test under station cyan, emergency red and amber strobe. Check that Kai's orange stays the warmest hue in frame.

These references go into every shot generation (see `08_generation_guide.md`).
