## Lessons from *Cargo* (and how they are used here)

1. **The infection is the clock.** Cargo's countdown lives inside the hero's body. → In *The Last Train*, the incubation rules (3 min to 6 h) let any bitten or scratched character carry a hidden timer (Marcus's scratch, Ep 4).
2. **Protecting a child is the strongest possible stake.** → Two nine-year-olds, Lucy and Wren, make every choice cost a child something.
3. **Quiet over gore.** Cargo is frightening without dialogue or splatter. → Hush infected are triggered by sound, so silence is both the survival rule and the aesthetic. Gore is restrained.
4. **One clear objective.** → Ep 1: reach the shutter by 2:21. Season: reach Lucy before 6:00.
5. **A payoff built from an early setup.** → The mop Kai carries through all of Ep 1 becomes the door bar in Ep 2A. Ruth's "You opened it" pays off in Ep 5.

## Short-form episodic craft (principles, not copied content)

| Technique | Why it works | How we use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame-1 hook** | Swipe decisions happen in the first second | Ep 1 opens mid-action: doors opening on a still crowd. No logo, no intro. |
| **A question in the first line** | An open loop pulls viewers forward | "Nobody got off." *Why not?* |
| **New information every 2–3 s** | Keeps attention without needing fast cuts | 30 shots in 88 s. Every shot adds a fact, a threat or a feeling. |
| **Earned pauses** | Contrast makes the scares land | Two full audio drop-outs. Two long shots (S21, S26) where nobody runs. |
| **Rules the audience can learn** | Viewers predict, worry and argue | The Hush sound rules are shown four times in Ep 1 and never explained aloud. |
| **A hero with flaws** | You can't care about someone who can't fail | Kai causes the danger (radio, keys) through ordinary panic. |
| **Moral-dilemma vote** | Comments become arguments, not "first!" | A/B is always a value clash: loyalty to Lucy vs. compassion for Wren. |
| **A recurring series device** | The format itself becomes recognisable | Kai looks into the lens and whispers the dilemma at the end of every episode. |
| **Ownership in the next episode** | Voters come back to see "their" outcome | Part 2 opens by stamping the result and *"You told me to open it."* |
| **Loopable ending** | Replays help watch time | The last sound (door chime) is the first sound. |
| **Clues for rewatchers** | Theory comments, rewatches | One hidden clue per episode (the board row 0417, "us", the bandage). |

## What we deliberately avoided

- Hero competence (no weapons skills, no parkour, no gun in Ep 1).
- Explaining the infection in Ep 1. It's shown, never told.
- Jump scares as the main tool. The biggest scare (S26) is a slow push and a silhouette.
- Generic identical zombies. Each featured infected has a silhouette, a speed and a "life before" detail.
- Any characters, designs, scenes or music from existing works.
