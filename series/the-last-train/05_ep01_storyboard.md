# THE LAST TRAIN — Episode 1 Storyboard (Stage 4)

30 shots · 88.0 s · 1080×1920 · 24 fps. Lens values are full-frame equivalents. "Push 0.2%/f" means the frame scales up 0.2% per frame (a slow creep).

**Global camera rules for this episode:**
- No two consecutive shots share an angle or lens.
- Every hold has a reason, or a slow creep.
- Shake happens only on impacts (S12), as a flip-sign kick that decays over 7 frames.
- Screen direction on the mezzanine: **threat = screen-left, exit = screen-right** (see bible §10).

**How to read each shot:**
1. Duration · 2. Camera position · 3. Camera movement · 4. Character position · 5. Character movement · 6. Expression · 7. Environment · 8. Lighting · 9. Dialogue/VO · 10. SFX · 11. Music · 12. Transition.
Then **GEN**: the shot prompt. Prepend the STYLE block and attach the character references from `08_generation_guide.md`. **All readable text** (signs, the board, phones, the door warning) is **added in post**, never generated.
