# THE LAST TRAIN — Series Bible (Stage 1: Story Development)

An interactive, animated zombie-survival series for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels.
Format: vertical 9:16, 75–90 s episodes, stylized premium 3D. Every episode ends with a two-option audience vote; the winning option becomes canon in the next episode.

---

## 1. Logline

At 2:17 AM a metro train pulls into Halloran Cross station and nobody gets off. Kai, a 23-year-old night cleaner, has four minutes before the city seals every station shut. His little sister is home alone four stops away, and a girl he has never met is begging him to open a chained door.

**Series promise:** *You don't watch the apocalypse. You steer it.*

## 2. Development answers (the eight Stage 1 questions)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Strongest opening hook** | Frame 1: train doors slide open on a full carriage. Every passenger stands with their back to the doors, faces pressed to the far windows, swaying. VO: *"The last train came in at 2:17. Nobody got off."* The picture does the hooking before a word is spoken. |
| **Protagonist's motivation** | Get home to his sister **Lucy (9)**, who is alone in their flat across the river while their mum works a night shift at St. Brigid's Hospital. Underneath that, he wants to be the person his dad wasn't, someone who doesn't leave. |

| **Central conflict** | Survival versus decency. Every choice sets *getting home* against *not leaving anyone behind*, and the city itself has chosen to bury the problem along with everyone underground. |
| **Emotional stakes** | Two nine-year-old girls, Lucy and Wren, mirror each other. Saving one always risks the other. |
| **Major revelation (Ep 1)** | Wren, the girl behind the chained door, says she's alone, but a very tall man in a train driver's cap is standing in the dark behind her, swaying. She's lying to protect him. |
| **Audience decision (Ep 1)** | **A: Open the door** (save Wren, likely get sealed underground). **B: Run for the shutter** (reach Lucy, leave a child behind). |
| **Part 2 possibilities** | Branch A, "Quiet": the chain clanks, the father wakes, and Kai and Wren are sealed below and must learn to survive in silence. ## 3. The world

**City:** an unnamed, English-speaking river city (deliberately unplaceable, so every viewer can imagine it as theirs). The **City Metro** has nine lines. **Line 7 (violet)** is the only line that runs *under* the river to Eastbank.

**Night 1 timeline (canon background, revealed piece by piece):**

| Time | Event (most of this is hidden from Kai) |
|---|---|
| 3 weeks ago | Kessler Deepworks, boring the Line 7 extension, breaches a sealed 1960s chamber under the river ("Line Zero"). Six workers fall sick. It's reported as a gas leak. |
| 23:40 | First "violent fever" patients reach St. Brigid's Hospital, where Kai's mum, Elena, is on shift. |
| 23:58 | Metro Control quietly schedules the emergency PA message for 02:17. *Someone knew two hours early.* (Theory seed.) |
| 01:40 | A bitten passenger boards Train 0412. Driver Tomas Hale, who has his daughter Wren in the cab against the rules, is bitten breaking up the "fight". |
| 02:05 | Tomas gets Wren off at Halloran Cross and hides with her in the Signal Equipment Room. Station supervisor Ruth Adeyemi finds them, sees the bite, chains the door with both of them inside and writes the warning. |
| **02:17** | The last train, 0412, arrives full of "Stillers". All services suspended. **Episode 1 begins.** |
| 02:21 | Station shutters seal under Control's lockdown order. |
| 03:00 | **Train 0417**, driverless and officially "not in service", begins "collecting" from sealed stations. |
| 06:00 | **Protocol Undertow:** the river floodgates open into the Line 7 tunnels. Everything underground drowns, infected and survivors alike. |

## 4. The infection: "The Hush"

Nobody official names it. At St. Brigid's the nurses started calling it **the Hush**, because the infected go quiet first. The rules are strict and consistent, so viewers can learn them, predict them and argue about them.

**Transmission:** bite, or infected blood into eyes, mouth or an open wound.

**Incubation depends on dose and location:**
- Neck or face bite: 3–5 minutes.
- Hand or limb bite: 30–60 minutes.
- Scratch: 2–6 hours, and not every scratch turns. This is what lets characters hide an injury.

**Stages:**

| Stage | Name | Look | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Fevered** | Sweat, nosebleed, flinching at sound | Still human, but every sound feels painfully loud (hyperacusis). |
| 1 | **Stillers** | Grey-green skin, dark vein webbing from the wound, milky eyes with pinprick pupils, **thin dark blood trails from both ears** (burst eardrums, the series' visual signature) | Stand motionless facing walls or windows, swaying, with a faint humming under the breath. They are *listening*. |
| 2 | **Bolters** | Same as Stillers, but jerky and stuttering, as if missing frames | They react to **new** sounds, not constant ones (an alarm that keeps beeping fades into the background). **A soft new sound → a twitch. A sharp new sound (radio, keys, glass, a ringtone, a scream) → they *orient*, turning toward it. A second sound from the same place → they *bolt*** in stuttering bursts. After **20–40 s of silence** they settle back into Stillers. Sound from everywhere at once (the PA) makes them tilt toward it, but they can't locate it. |
| 3 | **Conductors** | Hours after turning: skin ashen and cracking, jaw locked open, taller-seeming posture | Slow and relentless. They give off a low hum that **wakes every Stiller nearby** and pulls them along like a herd. Introduced mid-season. Conductors break the "quiet is safe" rule. |

**The in-world theory** (Priya, Ep 4): the Hush attacks the hearing and startle centres of the brain, so sound is agony and attraction at once. They attack the noise to make it stop.

**The survival rules viewers learn without exposition:**
1. Quiet keeps you alive. Whispers are safe.
2. **The rule of two:** the first sharp sound turns them, the second brings them.
3. If they bolt, freeze, and count to forty.
4. Trains are both escape and alarm bells, because the vibration wakes whole tunnels.

**Hidden rule (not revealed until Ep 6):** the infected **ignore Wren**. That's how she survived an hour locked in with her father, and why he calms when she touches him. Viewers will read it as love. It's immunity.

## 5. The central mystery (what viewers theorise about)

1. **Why did the last train keep running?** Train 0417 moves after every service is suspended. *Answer (Ep 6–8):* Control uses it to "collect" infected from sealed stations and carry them into Line Zero before the 6:00 AM flood.
2. **Who chained the door?** The handwriting on the warning matches Ruth's on the staff shift board (Ep 5). She did it on Control's orders, in exchange for a promised evacuation of her own family.
3. **Why didn't Wren turn?** She has an old, healed scratch on her wrist from visiting her dad's worksite three weeks ago. Low-dose early exposure made her **immune**, and the infected don't hunt the immune. Control wants her alive (Season 1 finale, Season 2 engine).
4. **What did Kessler Deepworks dig up?** A sealed Cold War containment chamber under the river: Line Zero.
5. **Where is Mum?** Elena Moreno never came home from St. Brigid's, ground zero of the hospital cases. Her fate is held back for the season finale.

**Clue placement in Episode 1** (so rewatchers can find them):
- Wren's slip: *"A lady locked **us** in."* (Ruth, and "us" means her dad.)
- The departure board: every line flips to SUSPENDED except one row that stays lit, `0417  NOT IN SERVICE`.
- The PA message's calm "Help is on the way" (it was pre-scheduled hours earlier).
- Lucy's voicemail: *"Mum's still at the hospital."*
- Wren's wrist bandage, visible for 8 frames in the window shot.

## 8. Branch map: how the votes reconnect

```
EP1  ── A: open the door ──► EP2A "Quiet" ──┬─ A: stop the train ─┐
     │                                       └─ B: hide ──────────┤
     └─ B: run ───────────► EP2B "Shutter" ─┬─ A: go back down ──┤──► EP3 "Collection"
                                             └─ B: cross city ────┘    (Train 0417, 3:00 AM)
```

All four Episode 2 outcomes arrive at **Train 0417 at 3:00 AM**. They meet in the same place but carry different **canon flags** forward:

| Flag | If EP1 = A (opened) | If EP1 = B (ran) |
|---|---|---|
| Wren's trust in Kai | **High.** She holds his hand and teaches him the rules. | **Broken.** She tells the group Kai left her. Marcus becomes her protector. |
| Tomas (the Tall Man) | Barred in with Kai's mop. Breaks free in Ep 3 and follows Wren's voice. | Released when Marcus cut the chain. Marcus was scratched doing it. Tomas trails Wren and never harms her. |
| Ruth | Sealed Kai below. Said *"You opened it"* (suspicious). | With Kai. Closer, so her secret hurts more when it surfaces. |
| Lucy | Text only. Kai couldn't call (noise), so she is scared and silent. | Voice contact and a promise made. Kai's **phone battery %** becomes a clock. |
| Kai's arc | Responsibility: he chose this child, now he has to keep her alive. | Guilt and redemption: he has to earn back a child's trust. |
| Resource | Wren's driver key (from her dad) | Ruth's master keycard and fire axe |

**Rule:** every later episode refers to at least one canon flag, so the vote never stops mattering.

## 9. Art direction: the look of the series

**Identity:** *"Sodium & Signal"*, a painterly, stylized 3D world. Sculpted, slightly exaggerated forms (bigger eyes and hands, simplified planes), hand-painted surface texture with visible brushwork in walls and tiles, subsurface skin, and cinematic lighting with real volumetric haze. Not photoreal, not flat 2D, not toy-like.

**Colour script:**

| Beat | Dominant light | Hex | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal station (opening) | Cold fluorescent cyan-white, teal-black shadows | `#CFEFEA` / `#0E1A1C` | Fragile normality |
| The turn (radio blast) | Fluorescents die, **emergency red** | `#D7262E` | The world has changed |
| The run | Red plus **amber shutter strobes** | `#FF9A1F` | Time running out |
| The door | Red, with a single cold practical in the window | `#D7262E` + `#9FD8FF` | The choice |
| Street level (Ep 2B) | Sodium amber rain | `#FFB347` | A worse world above |
| Under the river (Ep 4) | Near-black, phone light only | `#05080A` + `#EAF6FF` | Silence |

**Hero colour rule:** Kai's hi-vis orange (`#FF6A13`) is the *only* saturated warm colour in the underground. Viewers should be able to find him in any frame in under half a second.

**Signage (original, never a real network's):** City Metro uses a **circle split by a diagonal bar**. Station names are white condensed caps on a deep teal enamel band with brass trim. Line 7 is violet `#6A4C93`. Walls are cream bevelled tiles with a teal band at shoulder height.

## 10. Location: Halloran Cross (fixed geography for continuity)

```
LEVEL -1  TICKET HALL ── glass doors to Halloran Square (street, rain)
            │  ticket office (CCTV wall), gates
            ▲  EXIT STAIRS  ── [INNER STEEL SHUTTER at the top]
LEVEL -2  MEZZANINE CORRIDOR (60 m, low ceiling)
            LEFT end: escalator top ◄──────────────► RIGHT end: exit stairs
                          middle, north wall: SIGNAL EQUIPMENT ROOM (chained)
            ▲  ESCALATOR (stopped)
LEVEL -3  PLATFORM 2, Line 7 eastbound: the train sits here
```

**Screen direction rule (180° line):** on the mezzanine, *safety is always screen-right* (shutter, exit, Lucy) and *the threat always enters from screen-left* (escalator). Kai running left-to-right means "toward home". The Signal Room door is on the camera-facing north wall, so in every wide the choice sits *between* the two.

**Environment evolves across the episode:** clean and humming → lights dying → red → broken glass and spilled belongings → strobing, alarm and dust from the shutter.

## 11. Sound identity

- **The leitmotif is the door chime.** The metro's two-tone door chime (E5 → C5) opens Episode 1 and becomes the series theme, re-harmonised on detuned piano and low brass. Every episode starts and ends with it, so the end of each episode loops cleanly back into its first frame.
- **The PA voice is a character:** calm, female, automated, polite, and increasingly wrong as the night goes on.
- **Silence is a weapon:** each episode has at least one complete audio drop-out before a reveal.
- **The Stiller hum:** a quiet, many-voiced hum, slightly out of tune, that you only notice when it stops.
- Mix target: −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP. Dialogue first, then SFX, then music.

## 12. Format devices (what makes it a series, not a video)

1. **Frame-1 hook.** No logo, no intro. The first frame is already strange.
2. **Kai looks into the lens** in the last shot of every episode and whispers the dilemma (*"What do I do?"*). The audience is literally being asked.
3. **The vote result opens the next episode.** For example, `YOU CHOSE: A — 61%` stamped over the first shot for 1 second, followed by Kai's VO *"You told me to open it."* The audience owns the consequences.
4. **The two choices are always a value clash** (loyalty vs. compassion, safety vs. truth), never right vs. wrong.
5. **Loop ending:** the final sound is the door chime, which is also the first sound.
6. **Clue per episode:** one rewatchable detail per episode feeds the theory comments.
