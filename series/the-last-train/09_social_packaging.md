### Thumbnail / cover frame
- **Primary concept, "Nobody got off":** Kai in the foreground, lower-left, orange vest glowing under red light, looking back over his shoulder in fear. Behind him, the open train doors and the crowd standing with their backs turned, except **one face that has turned to look straight at the viewer** (milky eyes, ear-blood trails). ## Voting operations (so the audience can trust the vote)

1. **Window:** 48 hours after posting. Post Part 2 within 7 days so momentum doesn't fade.
2. **Count:** top-level comments whose first character is A or B (case-insensitive), across all platforms, one vote per account per platform. Screenshot the tally.
3. **Tie or near-tie (within 2%):** Kai "can't decide", so post a 10-second tie-breaker short (Kai at the door, the CLANK again) with a fresh 24-hour vote.
4. **Announce:** open Part 2 with `YOU CHOSE: A — OPEN THE DOOR · 61%` (or whatever the real number is; **never invent the result**). Optionally post a community or story update with the tally screenshot.
5. **Reward reasoning:** heart and reply to the best arguments *for both sides*. Optionally have "Kai" reply in character to a few comments ("you're right… I'm so scared").
6. **Never ignore the vote.** If production can't make one branch work, say so openly rather than quietly overriding the audience.

## Series-level packaging
- **Playlist / series name:** `THE LAST TRAIN (You Decide)`. Link every episode to it (YouTube "Related video", TikTok series playlist).
- **Profile bio line:** *"An animated zombie series where the comments decide who lives. 🚇"*
- **Release cadence:** one episode every 3–4 days during a season (48 h vote plus production). Consistency matters more than speed.
- **Retention metric to watch:** the share of viewers who reach S26 (the silhouette reveal, 1:09). If many drop before 0:23, the hook needs work. If many drop between 0:36 and 0:48, the run section is too long.
