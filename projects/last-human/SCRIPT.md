# YOU'RE THE LAST HUMAN ALIVE… BUT SOMETHING IS WATCHING YOU.

60 s vertical (1080x1920, 30 fps) psychological horror short.
2D hand-drawn young man (dark messy hair, big expressive eyes, rust-orange hoodie) inside a moody 3D world.
Palette: dark navy, muted purple, desaturated teal, warm orange accents. Volumetric fog, neon reflections.

The user's brief stopped in Scene 1 ("The camera performs a"). Scenes 1-7 below finish it in the same spirit:
second-person narration that follows the title, escalating from loneliness to being watched, ending on a twist.

## Scenes and shots

**Scene 1 — The hook (0-5 s), apartment at sunrise.**
Close-up of him asleep on the pillow, sunrise light through the blinds striping his face. The camera performs a slow
push-in; his eyes snap open on "wake". He sits up as the camera pulls back and rises, revealing the dark apartment,
dust in the light rays and the huge window over the city. Title text at the top.

**Scene 2 — The silence (5-12 s).**
2a: over-the-shoulder at the floor-to-ceiling window: a vast sunrise city, highways full of stopped cars, nothing moves.
2b: the TV glows teal with static; he stands in its light, slow tracking shot.

**Scene 3 — The search (12-22 s), empty city, overcast day into dusk.**
3a: wide establishing shot, low angle: he is tiny in the middle of a huge foggy avenue.
3b: tracking shot past a café: door open, a half-eaten dinner and a coffee cup in the foreground.
3c: a red traffic light in the foreground, abandoned cars below; dusk turns warm orange.

**Scene 4 — The cameras (22-32 s), night, neon, wet street.**
4a: he walks under purple and teal neon; a CCTV camera on a pole in the foreground.
4b: close-up: the camera slowly rotates to follow him, red light on.
4c: low wide: every camera on the buildings turns at once. He looks up, scared.

**Scene 5 — The reflection (32-42 s).**
5a: side tracking shot along a long dark shop window, his reflection walking with him.
5b: he stops. The reflection takes one more step, stops, turns its head toward him and smiles.

**Scene 6 — Home (42-52 s), apartment at night.**
6a: he slams the door and locks the chain and deadbolt.
6b: he sits on the bed in the dark; the phone buzzes and lights his face.
6c: over the shoulder: the photo on the screen shows him asleep, taken from the corner of this room.

**Scene 7 — The watcher (52-60 s).**
7a: close-up, his eyes go wide, he slowly turns to look over his shoulder.
7b: slow dolly into the dark corner. Two pale eyes open in the dark, then a thin smile.
7c: cut to black, title stinger.

## Sound
Low drone bed, sub-bass swells, a lonely detuned piano motif, distant wind, clock ticks, TV static, footsteps,
camera servo whirrs, glass tone, phone buzz, heartbeat in Scene 7, a hard stinger at the cut to black.
Voice stays on top; music ducks under it.
