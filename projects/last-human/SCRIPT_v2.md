# THE LAST HUMAN ALIVE — story refinement (v2)

Same character, sets, palette and 2D-in-3D style as v1. The changes are story, pacing and payoff.

## What is wrong with v1 (scene by scene)

| Time | v1 scene | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-5 s | Title card "…BUT SOMETHING IS WATCHING YOU" over eyes opening | The title gives away the premise and the ending in second one. No question is planted. | Open on the mystery instead (the camera that moves), title moves to the end |
| 5-12 s | Window, then TV static | Two shots with one message ("everyone is gone"). He only stands and stares; no reaction. | One shot, and he acts: hand on the glass, shouts "HELLO?", silence answers |
| 12-22 s | Avenue, café, red light | Ten seconds that repeat "the city is empty". The narration describes what we already see. Biggest drop-off point. | Compress to ~6 s and turn it into a clue: the coffee is still warm after three days, the light never changes. Time is frozen |
| 22-32 s | Cameras turn | First real escalation arrives at 23 s, too late. He is passive. | Move a camera shot to the cold open; here he tests it (steps left, steps right, it follows) before every camera turns |
| 32-42 s | Reflection takes one more step | Strongest scare, but it is a new threat that is never paid off. | Keep it, and make it the thread the ending pays off |
| 42-52 s | Home, phone, photo of him asleep | Good escalation, but nothing about the photo contradicts what he knows. | Add the contradiction: he has not slept since it happened |
| 52-60 s | Tall shadow creature in the corner | Generic monster; the ending is exactly what the title promised, so no twist. Cameras, reflection and photo never connect. | Replace with the payoff: someone is in his bed. It sits up. It is him |

Through-line problems: second-person narration keeps the viewer at a distance; the protagonist has no goal and almost
no reactions; every clue is a separate "something is watching" beat instead of one mystery that builds.

## v2 story: one mystery, one answer

The world froze when everyone vanished. Only two things still move: him, and whatever is watching him. The cameras,
the reflection and the photo are the same thing: a copy of him, learning to be him. The last shot repeats the
opening shot (the sit-up in bed) with the copy, so the loop asks: who woke up on day one?

First-person voice-over (more personal, one steady low tone), sparse: no line describes what is already on screen.

| Time | Shot | Voice-over / sound | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3.5 s | COLD OPEN: CCTV snaps round to stare into the lens, red light. Text: DAY 3 | "Everyone on Earth is gone. So who's moving the cameras?" | Hook: plants the question |
| 3.5-8 s | His eyes snap open; he jolts upright in the sunrise light (v1 1a + 1b) | "Three days ago, I woke up… and the world was silent." | Set-up |
| 8-13 s | At the window, hand on the glass, he shouts | His own voice: "HELLO?!" — it echoes back. Then nothing | Emotion: hope, then fear |
| 13-19 s | Fast: empty avenue; close on the café cup, steam still rising; red light | "Everything stopped when they vanished. The coffee is still warm." | Clue: time is frozen |
| 19-28 s | Night. He stops, steps left, steps right: the camera follows each step. Then every camera turns | "Nothing in this city moves anymore. Nothing… except me. And them." | Escalation + he acts |
| 28-38 s | Shop window: he stops, the reflection takes one more step, turns, smiles (v1 5a-5c) | "Then my reflection took one more step than I did." | Scare + the thread |
| 38-46 s | Home: chain lock; sitting on the bed, the phone buzzes | "I ran home. And for the first time in three days… my phone buzzed." | Escalation |
| 46-52 s | The photo: him asleep in this bed. Timestamp: NOW | "A photo of me, asleep. Taken tonight. But I haven't slept since it happened." | Contradiction |
| 52-58 s | He turns to the bed: a shape under the duvet, breathing. It sits up (same move as the opening): it is him, dead eyes, too-wide smile | "So who's sleeping in my bed?" | Twist that connects everything |
| 58-60 s | Glitch to black: PART 2? | Stinger | Cliffhanger + loop |

## Production notes
- Reused: apartment, skyline, city, café, CCTV, shop window, phone and photo, all character drawing and sound tools.
- New: cold-open camera shot, window shout pose, café steam, camera-follow test, the double in the bed.
- Captions move to a separate overlay pass so later text changes never need a 3D re-render.
- New voice-over lines and his shouted "HELLO?" are generated with the same local voice.
