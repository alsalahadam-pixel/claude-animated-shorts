# THE LAST HUMAN ALIVE — final screenplay and production plan (v2)

60.0 s film + 0.4 s black + 1.6 s PART 2? card = 62.0 s. Vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps (frame = seconds x 30).
Style unchanged from v1: same 2D character, hybrid 2D/3D, purple/orange/navy palette, same lighting language.
Status: **awaiting approval. Nothing is rendered yet.**

---

## 1. Story rules (continuity bible)

The viewer must be able to work these out, and no frame may break them.

1. **At 06:12 on Day 1, everything people left behind stopped.** Clocks read 06:12 forever, the coffee steam hangs
   motionless, a pigeon hangs mid-wingbeat, traffic lights stay red and queued cars keep their brake lights on.
   Street lamps, neon signs and lit windows stay exactly as they were: lit, never flickering.
   Dust in the light beams hangs still. There is no wind, no ticking, no hum of traffic.
2. **Only the sky still turns.** The sun rose on Day 1 and nights still fall. This is how he counts the days.
3. **Only two things move: him, and "them".** The cameras, the reflection, the message on his phone and the double are
   all "them". Every moving human-made thing in the film is one of these.
4. **He has not slept since 06:12 on Day 1.** This is said once, early (0:12), and never repeated. The photo pays it off.
5. **Deliberately unanswered (Part 2 hook):** who took the photo, if the double was asleep in it?

v1 details removed because they broke these rules: TV static, a ticking clock, neon flicker, wind gusts, blinking
hazard lights, moving dust, street lamps switched off in the daytime shots, and the tall shadow creature.

## 2. Screenplay

**COLD OPEN — EXT. CITY STREET — DAY 3, 02:41 — NIGHT**
Neon, wet asphalt, total stillness. A CCTV camera on a pole fills the foreground. It turns, slowly, to stare at an
empty patch of pavement. It waits. HE walks out of the darkness into exactly that spot, and looks up at it.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Everyone on Earth is gone. So who's moving the cameras?

**INT. APARTMENT — DAY 1, 06:12 — SUNRISE**
His eyes snap open. He jolts upright. The bedside clock reads 06:12. At the window he presses his palm to the glass.

> HIM: Hello?!

The city throws his voice back, smaller each time. Then nothing at all.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Three days ago, I woke up… alone. I haven't slept since.

**EXT. CITY — DAY 2**
An empty avenue; a pigeon hangs motionless in mid-air above him. At a café, steam hangs above a coffee cup like
glass. His fingers pass through it. It does not move. A traffic light glows red beside a clock that reads 06:12.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Everything they left behind stopped… the second they vanished.

**EXT. CITY STREET — DAY 3, 02:43**
Under the camera. He steps left: it follows. He steps right: it follows. Then every camera on the street turns to him.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Nothing here moves anymore. Nothing… except me. And them.

**EXT. SHOP WINDOW — CONTINUOUS**
His reflection walks with him. He stops and faces it. He raises a hand; it raises a hand. He tilts his head; it
tilts, a beat late. He lowers his hand; later still. He steps back and stops. It takes one more step. Silence.
It turns its head on its own, and smiles.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): It wasn't copying me. It was practising.

**INT. APARTMENT — DAY 3, 03:14 — NIGHT**
A door slams. The chain goes on. He slides to the floor. His phone buzzes for the first time in three days.
From: "Me". A photo of him, asleep in his bed. Sent 03:13.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): A photo of me… asleep. Taken one minute ago. So who's sleeping in my bed?

Across the dark room, the duvet rises and falls. Someone is breathing under it. It sits up, slowly. It has his
face. It looks straight at us and smiles.

CUT TO BLACK. **PART 2?**

## 3. Shot-by-shot production plan

Camera rule: locked-off shots with an imperceptible creep (0.1–0.2 % per frame, so nothing reads as a freeze).
Only three shots move: S12 (track), S18 (slow push on the phone), S20 (slow push toward the bed). All cuts are hard
cuts: no dissolves, no dips except the final black.
Caption rule: word-highlighted captions for narration (as v1); day/time labels in a small monospace style, top-left.
Reaction rule: every visual beat lands 0–3 frames before its word.

### ACT I — The question (0.00–4.80)

**S01 · 0.00–4.80 · f0–144 · Cold open, Day 3 street (night)**
- Camera: locked, low (1.2 m), 32 mm feel. CCTV on its pole large in the right third; empty wet street, neon behind.
- Action: 0.00–0.40 stillness. 0.40 the CCTV pans 70° left toward an empty spot at frame left, settles at 1.50,
  tilts down 1.50–1.70, red LED blinks once. Holds on nothing. 2.30 he walks in from the left edge into its aim,
  stops at 2.90, lifts his head to look at it (3.10). Expression: exhausted, dark under-eyes, wary; no blink.
- Label: "DAY 3 · 02:41" fades in 0.20–0.60, out at 3.00.
- V.O.: V01 "Everyone on Earth is gone." 0.50–2.61 · V02 "So who's moving the cameras?" 2.85–4.81
- Sound: low neon hum only. Servo whirr 0.40–1.50 with a mechanical stop. Two footsteps. Sub swell from 2.30;
  one high detuned piano note on his entrance.

### ACT II — Three days without sleep (4.80–13.40)

**S02 · 4.80–7.20 · f144–216 · Day 1, pillow close-up (sunrise)**
- Camera: locked top-down on his face; sunrise stripes from the blinds; push 0.2 %/f.
- Action: eyes closed; snap open 2 frames before "woke"; eyes go to the window 1 s later; one slow blink.
- Label: "DAY 1 · 06:12" 4.80–6.80.
- V.O.: V03 "Three days ago, I woke up… alone." 4.95–7.81 (runs over the cut).
- Sound: no ticking, no wind (rule 1). Faint high air tone. One low felt-piano note as his eyes open.

**S03 · 7.20–8.90 · f216–267 · He jolts upright**
- Camera: locked side view across the bed (v1 shot 1b without the crane move). Bedside clock in frame: red "06:12".
- Action: 7.30 jolts upright in 8 frames (ease-out, tiny overshoot), stares at the window. Dust in the light is frozen.
- Sound: sharp inhale, duvet rustle.

**S04 · 8.90–11.40 · f267–342 · The window: "HELLO?!"**
- Camera: locked over the shoulder; the frozen sunrise city fills the glass (no birds, nothing moving).
- Action: 9.00 he presses his right palm flat on the glass and leans in; 9.35 shouts (body lunges forward 6 frames).
- Dialogue: SHOUT "Hello?!" 9.35–10.22 (his voice, raw and strained; captioned large).
- Sound: echoes from the city 10.40 / 10.85 / 11.25, each quieter and more distant. Captions echo too:
  "hello…" smaller and fainter each time.

**S05 · 11.40–13.40 · f342–402 · His face at the glass**
- Camera: locked reverse close-up through the glass; orange sunrise on his face; his breath fog on the glass.
- Action: hope draining to fear; eyes glisten; jaw tightens on "slept".
- V.O.: V04 "I haven't slept since." 11.75–13.48
- Sound: 11.40–11.75 **total silence** (every bed muted). Then one low piano note under the line.

### ACT III — The rule (13.40–19.70)

**S06 · 13.40–15.00 · f402–450 · Day 2, the empty avenue (overcast)**
- Camera: locked high wide. He is tiny in the road. A pigeon hangs frozen mid-wingbeat in the air above him.
  Street lamps lit (pale in daylight); queued cars at the far light with brake lights on.
- Label: "DAY 2" 13.40–14.80.
- V.O.: V05 "Everything they left behind stopped…" 13.55–16.11 (runs over the cut).
- Sound: near silence: one footstep pattern, no wind, no traffic.

**S07 · 15.00–18.10 · f450–543 · The café cup: frozen steam**
- Camera: locked close-up on the cup and the half-eaten meal; shallow focus.
- Action: the steam is a sculpted curl hanging perfectly still. 15.50 his hand enters; 16.00–16.60 fingers pass
  through the steam; it does not move or part. 17.40 the hand withdraws slowly.
- V.O.: V06 "…the second they vanished." 16.35–17.95
- Sound: silence; a faint glassy tone while his fingers are inside the steam.

**S08 · 18.10–19.70 · f543–591 · The red light and the 06:12 clock (dusk)**
- Camera: locked low angle. Traffic light on red top of frame; a public clock on the building beside it reads 06:12;
  cars waiting below, brake lights on. He walks under it, back to us.
- Sound: the faint electric hum of the red lamp. No music.

### ACT IV — Them (19.70–28.30)

**S09 · 19.70–24.80 · f591–744 · Day 3: testing the camera (night)**
- Camera: locked medium wide; the same pole camera as S01, he stands beneath it looking up.
- Action: 20.20 one step left; 20.45–20.85 the camera follows. 22.70 one step right; 22.95–23.35 it follows.
  Each time he freezes and watches it. Expression: fear held down; breath visible in his shoulders.
- Label: "DAY 3 · 02:43" 19.70–21.20.
- V.O.: V07 "Nothing here moves anymore." 20.60–22.48 · V08 "Nothing… except me." 23.10–24.76
- Sound: a servo whirr exactly on each follow; low pulse (60 bpm) enters at 21.00.

**S10 · 24.80–27.10 · f744–813 · Every camera turns**
- Camera: locked low wide from road level; he stands in the middle of the street; cameras on poles, walls and
  traffic-light arms.
- Action: 24.80–25.10 nothing. 25.10–26.00 every camera turns to him at once (staggered by 2–3 frames).
- V.O.: V09 "And them." 26.00–27.05
- Sound: 0.3 s of silence (the pulse cuts out), then a wall of servo whirrs, panned around the frame.

**S11 · 27.10–28.30 · f813–849 · His face**
- Camera: locked close-up, low angle, purple neon key, teal rim.
- Action: eyes wide, pupils pinpoint, swallows; does not blink.
- Sound: breath only; heartbeat enters very low.

### ACT V — The reflection (28.30–43.70) — the centrepiece

**S12 · 28.30–30.60 · f849–918 · Walking with the reflection**
- Camera: slow side track matched to his walk (the act's only moving camera); the long dark shop window behind him.
- Action: the reflection walks in perfect sync.
- Sound: his footsteps with a faint glassy doubling; heartbeat low.

**S13 · 30.60–35.40 · f918–1062 · The mirror test**
- Camera: locked over his shoulder toward the glass; his back three-quarter in the foreground, the reflection
  facing us through the glass.
- Action (all slow, deliberate):
  - 30.60 he stops and turns to face the glass; the reflection turns in sync.
  - 31.60 raises his right hand; the reflection raises its hand in perfect sync.
  - 33.00 tilts his head; the reflection tilts **4 frames late**.
  - 34.00 lowers his hand; the reflection lowers it **10 frames late**.
  - 34.80 leans closer; the reflection holds still, then leans **14 frames late**.
- Sound: all ambience fades out by 31.20. Only his breathing and a thin high glass tone that slowly rises in pitch.

**S14 · 35.40–38.40 · f1062–1152 · One more step**
- Camera: locked side two-shot along the glass (reframed so the reflection is clearly separate from him).
- Action: 35.80 he steps back along the glass; the reflection mirrors. 36.30 he stops. 36.70–37.30 **the
  reflection takes one more step.** 37.90–38.30 its head turns toward us on its own; his body stays frozen.
- Sound: the glass tone cuts out on the step. **One wet footstep is the only sound.** Then dead silence to 38.40.

**S15 · 38.40–43.70 · f1152–1311 · The smile**
- Camera: locked close-up on the reflection through the glass, faint glass streaks.
- Action: his face exactly, but with dead eyes (no highlights) and pinpoint pupils. 38.80–40.00 the mouth slowly
  stretches into a too-wide smile with teeth. Holds, unblinking, to the cut.
- V.O. (close, quiet): V10 "It wasn't copying me." 39.90–41.59 · V11 "It was practising." 41.85–43.39
- Sound: a sub drop as the smile starts and a slow reversed swell under the lines. Hard-cut by the slam.

### ACT VI — The photo (43.70–60.00)

**S16 · 43.70–45.90 · f1311–1377 · Home: the chain**
- Camera: locked inside the apartment, dark; he has his back to us at the door.
- Action: the cut lands on the door slamming; 44.30–44.70 he fumbles the chain on; 45.00–45.80 slides down to sit on
  the floor, back against the door.
- Sound: SLAM on the cut, chain rattle and latch, then room tone only. No ticking (rule 1).

**S17 · 45.90–47.90 · f1377–1437 · The buzz**
- Camera: locked medium on him on the floor; the phone face-down in his hand.
- Action: 46.40 the phone buzzes; he flinches. 46.90 he turns it over; the screen light hits his face from below.
- Sound: two buzzes against the floor; his breath catches.

**S18 · 47.90–52.10 · f1437–1563 · The photo**
- Camera: slow push in on the phone until the photo fills the frame.
- On screen: status bar 03:14. From: **Me**. A photo of him asleep in this bed, taken from the corner of the room.
  "Sent 03:13".
- V.O.: V12 "A photo of me… asleep." 48.20–50.42 · V13 "Taken one minute ago." 50.60–52.31
- Sound: a low dread swell; heartbeat a little faster.

**S19 · 52.10–54.50 · f1563–1635 · He looks up**
- Camera: locked close-up, phone light on his face.
- Action: 52.50 his eyes lift from the phone first; 53.00 his head follows, slowly, toward the bed across the room.
- V.O.: V14 "So who's sleeping in my bed?" 52.60–54.50
- Sound: everything drops out except a thin held note that fades away by 54.50.

**S20 · 54.50–58.30 · f1635–1749 · The bed**
- Camera: his point of view across the dark room; very slow push toward the bed. Window glow purple and teal; the
  bedside clock still reads 06:12.
- Action: 54.50–55.70 the duvet rises and falls: two slow breaths. 55.70–58.30 the shape sits up, slowly and
  smoothly, with no jolt (the mirror image of his own sit-up in S03); the duvet slides off its shoulders.
- Sound: silence except the slow breathing from the bed, which is not his.

**S21 · 58.30–60.00 · f1749–1800 · The double**
- Camera: locked close-up, straight on.
- Action: his identical face: same hair, same hoodie, but dead eyes, pinpoint pupils, slightly paler skin; no blink,
  no breathing movement. 58.30–58.60 it turns its head to look straight into the lens. 58.70–59.50 the smile
  spreads, too wide. Holds 0.5 s. **Hard cut to black at 60.00.**
- Sound: a high cluster rises over the last second, then **all sound cuts at 60.00**.

**60.00–60.40 · f1800–1812 · Black.** Absolute silence.

**S22 · 60.40–62.00 · f1812–1860 · PART 2?**
- Small "THE LAST HUMAN ALIVE" above a large glitching "PART 2?", same style as v1.
- Sound: one glitch hit and a low tail.

## 4. Character animation notes
- **Him:** the v1 design unchanged (dark messy hair, amber eyes, rust hoodie, navy jeans). Three days without sleep
  shows in heavier under-eye lines and slightly lowered lids. Hand-drawn key poses with smooth in-betweens; blinks
  only where listed (fear means fewer blinks).
- **The reflection and the double:** identical drawing, but no eye highlights, pinpoint pupils, slightly cooler and
  paler skin, no breathing, no blinks, and movement that is perfectly smooth (no overshoot, no secondary motion).
  The wrongness is in the timing, not in a monster design.
- **New poses:** palm on glass and shout, facing the glass with hand raised, head tilt, stepping back, sliding down
  the door to the floor, sitting on the floor with the phone, the double rising from the bed.
